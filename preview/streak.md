# GitHub Streak Stats

> **View other styles:** [Badge Marquee](./badge-marquee.md) | [Icon Marquee](./icon-marquee.md) | [Top Languages](./top-langs.md) | [Contribution Snake](./snake.md)

Showcase your GitHub streaks and commit consistency with dynamic streak calculations and an animated flame effect.

This card automatically calculates your **Current Streak**, **Longest Streak**, and **Total Contributions** over the past year directly from your GitHub contribution calendar.

---
